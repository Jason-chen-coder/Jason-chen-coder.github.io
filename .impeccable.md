## Design Context

### Users
Recruiters, engineering managers, and technical collaborators evaluating a senior frontend engineer for complex product, cross-platform, hardware-adjacent, or AI application work.

### Brand Personality
Precise, systems-minded, quietly confident. The experience should feel like an engineer's field notebook: clear evidence, useful context, and no inflated claims.

### Aesthetic Direction
Warm editorial portfolio informed by https://zhu.eitech.edu.cn/zh/. Use ivory paper, dark brown ink, restrained brass, teal and indigo details, large Chinese Fangsong headings, fine dividers, a floating capsule navigation, and original engineering diagrams. Keep the reference site's calm rhythm, tactile controls and deliberate motion while expressing Jason's identity and actual projects. The homepage should connect open-source visualization tools, cross-platform products, connected devices and AI workflows.

Conceptual imagery uses a consistent series of generated porcelain miniatures with slate-blue enamel, champagne-brass paths, pale-oak bases and soft daylight. Keep their subjects tied to the actual project domains. Preserve real screenshots, video stills and product photographs as professional project evidence; retain labels and interaction feedback in HTML.

### Design Principles
- Lead with a specific positioning statement, not a broad technology list.
- Lead the open-source section with the four most-starred non-fork repositories, followed by two smaller entries and a separate current AI workflow focus. Label GitHub counts with their verification date.
- Retain the four professional project references and bounded contribution descriptions from the existing portfolio.
- Make complex systems scannable through labels, metrics, and clear visual structure.
- Keep the page fast, responsive, accessible, and easy to contact from any section.
- Keep every professional claim bounded by the resume; omit unsupported metrics and availability language.
- Preserve all content without JavaScript or motion, and use only local runtime assets. Default to Chinese with a complete English switch.
- Build original code and illustrations; credit the design reference and retain open-source font licenses.
