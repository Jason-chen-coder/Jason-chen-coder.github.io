# Generated ceramic illustration series

Generated on 2026-11-01 with the built-in `image_gen` tool. Mode: original generation guided by style references; no CLI/API fallback was used. Deployed assets are 1200 × 800 WebP derivatives of the generated 1536 × 1024 PNG masters. Only resizing and WebP encoding were performed after generation.

## Art direction and references

The material, palette, lighting and miniature product-render treatment were studied from [Zhu Group](https://zhu.eitech.edu.cn/zh/). The image subjects are original interpretations of Jason's engineering work. They are conceptual illustrations, not screenshots or representations of the actual applications.

Style references supplied to the generator:

- [Porcelain terrain and compute module](https://zhu.eitech.edu.cn/assets/v12-imagery/research-theory-480.webp)
- [Automation workstation on a light wood plinth](https://zhu.eitech.edu.cn/assets/v12-imagery/research-ai-480.webp)
- After the first image was generated, that original flow-editor image was supplied as a style reference to keep the remaining images consistent.

The four existing professional project images, their source manifest, and brand marks were retained. Previous project SVGs remain on disk as recoverable sources.

## Shared prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: final editorial illustration for a personal engineer portfolio, part of one coherent five-image series. Style reference inputs are ONLY references for materials, camera, lighting, and tactile finish; create new subject matter, do not reproduce their chemistry equipment. Build a sophisticated physically rendered miniature object scene, not a screenshot, not flat vector art, not line-art, not a picture of a website. Match the supplied references: ivory glazed porcelain housings, restrained slate-blue enamel details, thin polished champagne brass rails, very pale natural oak plinth with softly rounded corners, precision mechanical details, gentle contact shadows. Background is solid warm ivory #f6f4ef, seamless tabletop and backdrop with no horizon line. Soft large-source daylight from upper left, matte paper softness, restrained specular metal and ceramic highlights. Three-quarter front view slightly above, calm orthographic-like perspective, whole subject visible, horizontal 3:2 composition, object centered in middle 72 percent, generous quiet margins including lower 12 percent for an HTML caption outside the image. No people, hands, neon, saturated colors, purple, cyberpunk, heavy dark surfaces, borders, badges, captions, watermarks, brand logos, or legible words/letters. These are conceptual illustrations; do not invent a real application screenshot.

## Delivery

The prompt for each asset is the shared prompt followed by its subject prompt. Each asset was generated in a separate built-in image generation call. The deployed files, dimensions and hashes are recorded in `generation-record.json`. No text is baked into the generated artwork; the website provides labels and translations in HTML. The code-editor image uses only punctuation marks as a visual cue.
